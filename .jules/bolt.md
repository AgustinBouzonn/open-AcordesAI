## 2025-08-21 - Resolving N+1 correlated subqueries with LATERAL JOINs
**Learning:** In the `songs.ts` backend routes (`/popular` and `/`), there was a severe performance bottleneck where left joins and subqueries in the SELECT clause were being used on fully aggregated tables (e.g. `(SELECT song_id, AVG(score) ... FROM ratings GROUP BY song_id)`). This was forcing Postgres to scan and aggregate the entire `ratings`, `favorites`, `history`, and `chord_cache` tables before performing the join, completely bypassing indexes on `song_id`.
**Action:** When resolving PostgreSQL N+1 correlated subqueries, use `LEFT JOIN LATERAL` instead of standard `LEFT JOIN` on fully aggregated subqueries or subqueries in the `SELECT` clause to preserve index usage and push down the outer filter conditions (like `WHERE song_id = s.id`).
