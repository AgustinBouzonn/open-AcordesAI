## 2023-10-27 - Icon-only buttons accessibility and empty states
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons need `aria-label` attributes to be accessible to screen readers, and `title` for visual tooltips. Also, empty states without an immediate call to action (like "create your first one") create friction for new users.
**Action:** When adding icon-only buttons, always include `aria-label` and `title`. Ensure empty states provide a quick action to resolve the empty state if applicable.
