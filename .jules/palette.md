## 2026-07-05 - Tooltips for Disabled Interactive Elements
**Learning:** Disabled icon buttons (like 'Favorite' or 'Rate' in SongViewer) can be confusing when the reason for disablement (e.g. needing to log in) isn't communicated.
**Action:** Add dynamic `title` tooltips to disabled interactive elements explaining why they are disabled (e.g., "Inicia sesión para valorar"), and ensure all icon-only buttons have descriptive `aria-label`s.
